## 1. Household and joint taxation

- **Joint taxation.** Married couples and declared partners are taxed jointly. The form has two columns, "contribuable" and "conjoint/partenaire", and most items are entered per person.
- **Household members.** The number of people in the household (taxpayers plus children in the household) multiplies several ceilings: insurance, home savings, mortgage interest.
- **Children.** Each child is listed with whether they lived under the same roof and whether family allowances (CAE) were received.

## 2. Salaries: reading the salary certificate

The employer's "Certificat de salaire, de retenue d'impôt et de crédits d'impôt bonifiés" has numbered lines. Copy them into the salary page of each employment:

| Line | Content | In the form |
|---|---|---|
| 2–6 | Gross remuneration by nature (salary, benefits in kind such as "SI", prime participative) | One row per nature |
| 8 | Total social contributions | "Cotisations sociales" |
| 9–10 | Non-deductible contributions, incl. those on the exempt part of a prime participative | "Cotisations sociales non déductibles" |
| 11 | Deductible contributions (8 − 9 − 10) | Computed by the form; check it matches |
| 12–16 | Deductions from the tax card (FD = travel costs, etc.) | Enter FD as "Frais de déplacement"; the others are applied by the tax office |
| 17–27 | Exemptions (overtime, supplements, other: e.g. 50 % of a prime participative, SI) | "Déclaration d'exemptions" = Oui, one row each |
| 28 | Remuneration used for withholding | Information |
| 29 | Tax withheld | "Retenue d'impôt" |
| 30–32 | CIS, CI-CO2, CIM tax credits | Same fields |
| 33 | CISSM | Not declared (the form says so) |
| 34 | Assurance dépendance | **Not deductible; no field.** Shown for information only. |

- **Frais d'obtention.** The flat-rate minimum (540 € per employed person in 2025) applies unless actual costs are higher and documented.
- **Several employers in a year.** Add one salary block per employer and period.

## 3. Income from capital

- **Dividends from a fully taxable resident capital company** (including an S.à r.l.-S) are **50 % exempt** (art. 115 no. 15a L.I.R.). Enter the gross amount on the "Revenus exemptés à 50 %" page per shareholder, in proportion to the shares held.
- **Dividend withholding.** The 15 % withholding is credited. Keep proof that the company paid it (withholding declaration and bank debit).
- **Year of a dividend.** The year it was decided and made available. If the decision and payment fall in different years, flag it for the accountant.
- **First slice exempt.** The first 1,500 € of capital income taxed by assessment is exempt, doubled for a jointly taxed couple (art. 115 no. 15). The form fills the "Tranche exemptée" itself.
- **Frais d'obtention.** The form proposes a flat-rate minimum (50 € for a couple in 2025) unless actual costs are declared.
- **Interest from Luxembourg paying agents** that has suffered the 20 % final withholding (RELIBI) is **not declared**; the categories page says so.
- **Interest credited on a home-savings deposit** (Bausparkasse approved in Luxembourg or the EU/EEA) is **exempt** (art. 115 no. 15 L.I.R.) when the savings finance the owner's own home. **Do not declare it** as capital income. Its annual statement may say "einkommensteuerpflichtig": that is German boilerplate and does not apply to Luxembourg residents.
- **Foreign capital income** is declared; tax credits come from the double tax treaty or the annex. *(verify per country)*

## 4. Owner-occupied homes and mortgage interest

Mortgage interest is declared under "Revenu net provenant de la location de biens", one block per home (rental value regime).

- **When the clock starts.** It starts when the rental value is fixed (circular L.I.R. no. 105/8 – 98/1 of 18 August 2023):
  - for an **existing dwelling**, from the **purchase deed**, even if it is being renovated or is unoccupied;
  - for a building **under construction or bought off-plan (VEFA)**, from **completion**;
  - for a building **unfit to live in** (no heating, basic sanitary facilities, kitchen point or room for shelter), from the end of the works. **Before** that, interest and loan-opening costs are deductible **in full**.
- **Ceiling on interest** (from tax year 2024, per person in the household):
  - deductible **in full** in the year the rental value is fixed and the following year;
  - **4,000 €** from the second year after that, for four years;
  - **3,000 €** for the next five years;
  - **2,000 €** after that.

  The form computes the ceiling and splits it per spouse. **It is not pro-rated** for a part-year.
- **Secondary residence.** Interest linked to a secondary residence is not deductible. When a family owns two homes in the same year, the home they no longer live in (old home awaiting sale) stops qualifying from the move date.
- **Interest follows the use of the money.** A refinancing loan keeps the character of the debt it repaid. A bridge loan (crédit relais) that partly repays the old home's loans and partly finances the new home is split by those uses. This is a judgement call worth an accountant's confirmation, because the old home is usually capped and the new one is not.
- **Financing costs.** Notary fees for the mortgage deed, mortgage registration and bank file fees are deductible as "frais de financement". Purchase costs (notary fees for the sale, registration duties) are not.
- **Part-year layout.** For a part-year home ("Librement disponible pendant une partie de l'année") the form asks, per loan:
  - the loan start date;
  - interest **before** availability;
  - interest **during** availability.

  When a certificate covers both periods, split the interest by days and say it is an estimate.
- **"Did 100 % of the debt serve to acquire this property?"** The form asks this for each loan. Answer truthfully. If part of a loan financed another property, it does not belong 100 % to this one.

## 5. Special expenses (dépenses spéciales)

- **Insurance premiums.** Life, death (including outstanding-balance loan insurance), accident, invalidity, health, civil liability; for vehicles only the deductible liability part. Together with personal-loan interest they are capped at **672 € per household member**, e.g. 5 persons = 3,360 €. Choose the risk type per contract. The insurer's fiscal certificate gives the deductible amount.
- **Home-savings contributions** to an approved Bausparkasse (Luxembourg, EU or EEA) are capped at **672 € per household member**, or 1,344 € for a taxpayer aged 18–40 at the start of the contract *(verify)*. Credited interest may be added to the contributions (guichet.lu). It rarely matters, because the ceiling usually binds. The contract details are pre-filled from last year.
- **Compulsory social contributions** are carried over automatically from the salary pages.
- **Other headings** have their own ceilings: old-age provision under art. 111bis or a PEPP, complementary pension schemes, donations to approved bodies. *(verify)*

## 6. Extraordinary charges (charges extraordinaires)

- **Flat-rate allowance** for domestic help, care for dependency or **childcare**: actual costs up to a yearly cap (**5,400 €**, i.e. 450 €/month *(verify)*).
  - Childcare covers children under 14 *(verify)*: crèche, maison relais, foyer scolaire, approved childminder.
  - Choose the type **"Frais de garde d'enfant"** for these invoices. Last year's pre-fill may say "Frais de domesticité", so check it.
  - Enter one entry per child, with the period and the annual amount actually paid.
- **Other headings:** costs under art. 127 (unavoidable charges), invalidity and children outside the household are rarer; ask if relevant.

## 7. Automatic or not declared

- **Applied by the tax office:** the extra-professional abatement for a two-earner couple (4,500 €) and the childcare allowance (not in the "revenu imposable" recap).
- **Not declared:** the assurance dépendance, the CISSM, interest under RELIBI final withholding and exempt home-savings interest.
- **No tax estimate in the form.** The recap page links to the official simulator for an estimate.
