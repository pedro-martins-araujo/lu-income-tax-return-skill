# Interview questions

Ask only what the documents and last year's return don't already answer. Ask a few at a time, explain briefly why each matters, and record the answer with its date (workbook open items or memory).

## Household
- Same marital or partnership status as last year? Any change (date)?
- Children: any birth, departure, change of custody? Did each child live with you all year?
- Should one of you be the "contribuable" again (same as last year)?
- E-mail and phone for each spouse (they are asked on the form)?

## Where you lived
- Did you move during the year or after it? **Actual** move date versus the date in the **commune register** (look for a residence certificate). The return should state one coherent story.
- Did you own more than one home at any point in the year?

## Homes and loans
- Bought, sold, built or renovated a home? Dates of deed, start and end of works, move-in, sale.
- Was the new home habitable when bought (heating, bathroom, kitchen), or only after the works? This decides when the interest ceiling starts.
- Any refinancing, restructuring, bridge loan? Which loan paid off what, and which loan financed which home?
- Notary and bank costs linked to a loan (mortgage deed, mortgage registration, file fees)?

## Income
- Employers and periods for each person; any change of job?
- Your own company: dividends decided in the year? Paid? Withholding tax paid, and when?
- Interest, dividends or rental income abroad? Pensions? Self-employed or occasional income?
- Anything sold: shares, a property other than your home?

## Deductions
- Insurance contracts (life, death / loan insurance, accident, health, civil liability, vehicle)?
- Home-savings contracts: still paying? Paid out, and used for what?
- Old-age provision (111bis / PEPP), complementary pension, donations?
- Childcare costs per child; domestic help or care costs?

## Practice and history
- Anything your accountant or a tax audit told you to do in a particular way?
- Anything you did last year you want to do differently this year?
